# [3] Federated Learning for Privacy-Preserving Intrusion Detection in Multi-Tenant Cloud
**Authors:** Y. Li, X. Li, D. Wu, F. Mtenzi, B. Alsinglawi (IEEE Transactions on Cloud Computing, 2023)
- **Architecture**: Federated Averaging + FedProx regularization + Gaussian Differential Privacy (epsilon=0.5).
- **Results**: 96.3% Federated accuracy on non-IID tenant splits vs 98.1% centralized baseline (1.8% privacy gap).
- **Project Role**: Blueprint for Layer 5 multi-tenant federated training.
