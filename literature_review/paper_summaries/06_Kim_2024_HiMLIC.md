# [6] Hi-MLIC: Hierarchical Multilayer Lightweight Intrusion Classification
**Authors:** Y. Kim, J. Kim, D. Kim (IEEE Access, 2024)
- **Architecture**: 3-layer hierarchy (binary -> 4 threat groups -> 23 attack subtypes).
- **Results**: 98.81% accuracy, 98.80% recall across consolidated CM datasets.
- **Project Role**: Attack taxonomy hierarchy and CM dataset preparation.
