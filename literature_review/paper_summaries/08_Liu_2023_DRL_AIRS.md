# [8] Reinforcement Learning-Based Adaptive Intrusion Response System
**Authors:** L. Liu, T. Ran, J. Liu, F. Xia, R. Li (IEEE T-Cloud, 2023)
- **Architecture**: DQN agent trained in Markov Decision Process simulation with Prioritized Experience Replay.
- **Results**: 91.3% mitigation effectiveness with 23% fewer false actions.
- **Project Role**: Layer 5 autonomous response engine.
