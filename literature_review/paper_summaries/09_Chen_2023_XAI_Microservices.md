# [9] XAI-Enhanced Intrusion Detection for Cloud-Native Microservices
**Authors:** J. Chen, S. Xiong, Y. Sun, K. Li (IEEE TSC, 2023)
- **Architecture**: LSTM Autoencoder + SHAP Tree/DeepExplainer on Istio service mesh traces.
- **Results**: 92.4% precision, 340ms SHAP generation latency, 47% reduction in analyst MTTI.
- **Project Role**: Layer 4 XAI explanation generation and SOC dashboard integration.
