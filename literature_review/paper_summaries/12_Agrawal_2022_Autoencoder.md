# [12] An Autoencoder-Based Approach for Anomaly Detection in Cloud Traffic
**Authors:** S. Agrawal, A. Kumar, P. Shah, R. Patel (IEEE Access, 2022)
- **Architecture**: Deep Denoising Autoencoder using reconstruction error thresholds.
- **Results**: 94.7% zero-day detection rate with 3.2% FPR.
- **Project Role**: Zero-day / novel attack detector in Layer 3.
