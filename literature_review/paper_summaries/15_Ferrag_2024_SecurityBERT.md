# [15] SecurityBERT: Privacy-Preserving BERT Model for IoT/Cloud Gateways
**Authors:** M. A. Ferrag et al. (IEEE Access, 2024)
- **Architecture**: BERT with Privacy-Preserving Fixed-Length Encoding (PPFLE).
- **Results**: 98.2% accuracy on 14 attack classes in a 16.7 MB model.
- **Project Role**: Edge/gateway lightweight intrusion detection.
